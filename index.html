Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

Portal submissions are stored securely and only used to coordinate service, scheduling, and billing. Photos and documents shared during a project remain private and are removed upon request after work is complete.

  • No resale of customer information
  • Encrypted portal communication
  • Documentation archived for 12 months

Terms & Conditions

Scope and expectations

Diagnostics are billed separately from repair labor. Parts are sourced once estimates are approved inside the portal. Travel fee applies to every visit outside Minneapolis / St. Paul city limits and is waived for multi-day projects.

  • Payment due upon delivery of documentation
  • Workmanship warranty for 30 days
  • Partner referrals billed directly
